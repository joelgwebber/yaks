A human's call (it edits another repo's farm, and some notes cite an image by link). Options: leave as is; or a one-off script that removes each `![..]` line whose file the yak's evidence document already embeds, keeping the files.

Done in the surveyor farm (Joel: "go ahead and just change those"). A one-off script, [attached](yaks-9173/unlink_surveyor.py), removed each `![..](<id>/<file>)` line from a yak's body when an evidence document that the yak already links embeds that file. All files are kept; only the redundant body lines go.

- **Result:** 126 links removed from 11 yaks (`survey-0419`, `-1616`, `-1a81`, `-4854`, `-5a14`, `-7874`, `-97c2`, `-a086`, `-a23e`, `-a520`, `-cf0d`); each keeps its link to the evidence document. A dry run came first and I read one diff in full (`survey-cf0d`).
- **Left alone:** 8 yaks whose evidence document is not linked or embeds none of their images (`survey-4854` keeps 6 images its document does not embed, 5871, 5916, 86a7, bda0, ce46, e509, f9ef, fcd5 have no evidence document), and `survey-cc60`, whose three documents are not linked from its body.
- **Check:** `yaks doctor` in that farm reports the same 9 broken links before and after (they are the old sibling-image links the census found, not these), so no link was broken.
- **Committed in the surveyor farm** (`yaks commit`), **not synced**: that is Joel's call.
