Follow-up to the yaks-38dc TUI plan (approach C in the 2026-10-12 brainstorm, deferred by Joel): an activity log view across every shed, newest first, i.e. `yaks log` over all sheds' farms: timestamp, actor, yak, first line, with the shed named. Arrival signals (a new ask in any shed flashes in the status bar) are part of approach B, not this. Build only after B and the Sheds tab (A) have been used for a while; decide then whether it is a view or a watch mode.
