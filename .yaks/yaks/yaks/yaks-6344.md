From the memcraft cloud-runner yakherd's report (2026-11-01). The `yaks-coordinating` and `yaks-working` skills need a short "on a cloud runner" section:

- **Wipes.** Only the harness worktree survives (that runner was wiped 4 times). Keep a repo setup script that restores tools in minutes, launched detached. Sync the farm after every claim and landing. A worker whose latest note is over an hour old after a reboot is dead: re-brief a **fresh** worker name, rather than reusing the dead one's.
- **Long jobs.** Launch builds as their own terminal command (`setsid nohup sh script > log 2>&1 < /dev/null &`) and poll at most every 10 minutes. Chained after other commands, they sometimes never start.
- **Disk.** Each Rust worker checkout's `target/` is 8–13 GB, and the runner filled up once. Budget concurrency by disk and CPU, and delete landed workers' build dirs.
- **Evidence.** Re-run workers' evidence when the machine is quiet: headless checks failed under load with three workers building. Briefs require the exact command and environment of every evidence run. The herd's re-run "failed" by aiming at water until it used the worker's recorded parameters.
- **Liveness without thread tools.** Thread tools may be unavailable there, so note cadence is the only liveness signal.

Keep it brief and harness-neutral where possible. The GLIBC noise (`yaks-de7a`) and farm bootstrap (`yaks-b46b`) are tooling yaks, not skills text.
