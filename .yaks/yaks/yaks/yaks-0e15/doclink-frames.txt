                                                │ The farm as a knowledge store
                                                │
                                                │ A farm holds working knowledge next to the work: yaks (what to do), pages (what we know),
                                                │ sources (what we read) and schemas (what shape typed facts take). It is all markdown with
                                                │ frontmatter on the farm branch, and every write is its own commit. Built under yaks-44b3
                                                │ in November 2026; this page records the model and why it is shaped this way.
                                                │
