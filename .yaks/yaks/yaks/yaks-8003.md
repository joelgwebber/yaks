Option (b) from the 2026-10-31 sheds discussion, parked until we have evidence. Joel: start with the `YAKS_ACTOR` rule (sibling skills yak) and resolve this with data from further experiments, including other harnesses such as Indent.

## The problem

Harness-derived actor names are unstable:

- A Delta thread writes as `delta:<thread id>` before it has a title, then `delta:<title>` (seen in the memcraft farm).
- Titles are cut at 40 characters (`actor::MAX_HARNESS_NAME`) and change when a thread is renamed.
- Two threads can share a title.

## The idea

Stamp a short stable id alongside the name, e.g. `▸ <ts> [delta:Set up remote yak workflow #ksQQOn_l]`, and group sheds rows by the id. A small note-format change; old stamps stay valid. If structured comment metadata lands (sibling design yak), the id belongs there instead.

## Evidence to gather

- How often the split happens in real farms (actors per thread).
- What identity other harnesses expose in the environment (Indent, Claude Code, Zed agents, Codex), if any. A new one is one line in `actor::HARNESSES`.
