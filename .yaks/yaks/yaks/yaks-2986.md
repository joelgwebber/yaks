Part of the scheduling arc ([design](/doks/design/scheduling.md)). `CreateForm` (`src/tui/create.rs`) holds only title, tags, source and description, so the TUI shows `Due:` and `Every:` but cannot set them.

- Due and Every rows on the create/edit form (blank clears; same parsers as the CLI, so `+2d` works).
- A `Cmd` in `keymap.rs` to snooze the selected yaks (prompt: `+1d`, `+1w` or a date), through the shared parser, bound with a help line so `?` and the hint list it.
- `docs/tui.md` updated; a docshot scene or insta snapshot of the form and the prompt, looked at.
