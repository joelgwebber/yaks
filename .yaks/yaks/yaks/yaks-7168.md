Five points on [the reflection proposal](/doks/design/session-reflection.md), each with my lean:

1. **Where lessons live:** one farm-wide `doks/lessons.md`. Alternatives: one per herd (`<herd>/doks/lessons.md`), or no dok at all and only proposals for `AGENTS.md`. Lean: farm-wide, since most farms are one project; a herd can grow its own later.
2. **Closing the loop:** the `yaks` skill tells agents to read `/doks/lessons.md` at session start when it exists. Lean: yes, one line. Without it the dok is write-only for anyone not running Hearth.
3. **Instruction files:** reflection never edits `AGENTS.md` or skills; it files at most one proposal per round as a yak awaiting you, with the text. Lean: yes.
4. **Threshold:** a lesson enters the dok when two sessions taught it, or one `failed`/`partial` turn did. Lean: yes; one-off lessons stay in their note.
5. **Mechanics and cadence:** a third weekly recurring yak, and `yaks session list --since <date>` once yaks-3f35's session changes land. Lean: yes.

If you agree, I'll write the procedure into `yaks-upkeep`, add the one line to `yaks`, and file the `--since` yak.
