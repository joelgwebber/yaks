Part of the scheduling arc ([design](/doks/design/scheduling.md)).

- `--due` on `create` and `update` accepts `+Nd` / `+Nw` (relative to today, as a date) besides a date or RFC 3339 datetime. Durations only: no weekday words.
- `yaks snooze <id>... <+Nd|+Nw|date>` moves `due:` and appends a `kind: snooze` note, so a snooze is visible in the history. Refuses a shorn or dead yak.
- Share the parser with the TUI snooze key (a sibling yak); put it in `recur.rs`.
