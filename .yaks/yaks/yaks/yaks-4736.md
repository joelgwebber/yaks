From yaks-7c6e, item 6 (Joel: "we want parity where appropriate, but that will require a careful evaluation"). edtui has about 164 vim bindings and 46 emacs ones; once yaks-7c6e makes the family a whole-TUI choice (`keys: vim|standard`), the standard family's editor is the thin one.

- List edtui's vim bindings by intent (motion, edit, select, search, undo, clipboard) and mark which have a standard/emacs equivalent.
- Decide, per missing intent, whether standard mode should have it and on which key (common-editor keys first: Ctrl-Z/Ctrl-Shift-Z, Shift-arrows select, Ctrl-arrows by word, Home/End, Ctrl-Home/End).
- Implement the chosen ones in the edtui fork (`yaks-integration` branch), then `cargo update -p edtui`.
- The `F1` editor popup (yaks-7c6e) lists them.
